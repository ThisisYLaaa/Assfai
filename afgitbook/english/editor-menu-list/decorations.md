# Decorations

<figure><img src="https://484108807-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fipb5NkFjq0eBOIGewqlQ%2Fuploads%2Fga1jmEQ70KhQzkPaL6Ru%2Fimagem.png?alt=media&#x26;token=d4a31301-6884-41bd-b2bc-194ac0a5baaf" alt=""><figcaption><p>Decorations - Add Decor / Text</p></figcaption></figure>

On the bottom of the <mark style="color:orange;">Decorations</mark> tab, you can see two buttons. The one with a tree is to **add decorations**, the other with an <mark style="color:orange;">**`Aa`**</mark> is to **add Text**

## How to add Decorations

Click on the **Add Decoration** button, a new tab on the right should appear, as well as a new decor on the <mark style="color:orange;">Decorations</mark> List

<figure><img src="https://484108807-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fipb5NkFjq0eBOIGewqlQ%2Fuploads%2F721KlGYzKbmJrQXnqBuh%2Fimagem.png?alt=media&#x26;token=3ce0e7cc-5a8e-4a13-840b-d993c60217be" alt="" width="370"><figcaption><p>Decorations - Default decor status</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="https://484108807-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fipb5NkFjq0eBOIGewqlQ%2Fuploads%2FR2NlITNjAZNUKTZRjHEC%2Fimagem.png?alt=media&#x26;token=241e9bdd-8070-44ec-8323-0d2a2891f6ef" alt="" width="337"><figcaption><p>Decorations Right Tab - Image Settings</p></figcaption></figure>

From here, you can upload your image. Your **decor** should appear on the level now!

{% hint style="info" %}
For more information on how to use <mark style="color:orange;">Decorations,</mark> check the <mark style="color:purple;">Advanced Techniques -</mark> [Decoration and Text Creative Format](https://adofaieditor.gitbook.io/english/event-skillful-formatting-and-showcases/decoration-and-text-creative-format) page
{% endhint %}

## How to add Text

Click on the **Add Text** button, and a new tab on the right should appear, as well as a new text on the <mark style="color:orange;">Decorations</mark> List

<figure><img src="https://484108807-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fipb5NkFjq0eBOIGewqlQ%2Fuploads%2Fn06Yqt7Qbac4DjJ3Ibbt%2Fimagem.png?alt=media&#x26;token=ad61c806-e1f8-4f22-b455-f951467d31cd" alt="" width="342"><figcaption><p>Decorations - Default text status</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="https://484108807-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fipb5NkFjq0eBOIGewqlQ%2Fuploads%2FLtnuabOMcw30jAepy1oa%2Fimagem.png?alt=media&#x26;token=f2e955cf-16af-4295-b2d4-071a47ce3675" alt="" width="341"><figcaption><p>Decorations Right Tab - Text Settings</p></figcaption></figure>

The word **Text** should appear on your level now, go over the <mark style="color:orange;">text</mark> area and change it to whatever text you want and done!

{% hint style="info" %}
For more information on how to use <mark style="color:orange;">Decorations Texts,</mark> check the <mark style="color:purple;">Advanced Techniques -</mark> [Decoration and Text Creative Format](https://adofaieditor.gitbook.io/english/event-skillful-formatting-and-showcases/decoration-and-text-creative-format) page
{% endhint %}
